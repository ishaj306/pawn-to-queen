# Pawn to Queen Design Language

Pawn to Queen is not a SaaS dashboard, gaming UI, or Chess.com clone. 

It should feel like:
- A luxury chess journal
- Vintage chess manuscripts
- Editorial magazines
- Royal and timeless
- Elegant and collectible

Inspired by:
- Hermès
- Ralph Lauren
- Premium books
- Beautiful journals
- Classical chess illustrations

---

## Typography
* **Preserve the current imported font system and typography.** Do not replace the existing font styling.
* The current heading fonts and spacing are part of the identity and should remain consistent across all pages.
* Maintain elegant typography hierarchy and generous whitespace.

---

## Colors

### Primary Palette
* **Primary Background:** Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F2`)
* **Primary Accent:** Emerald Green (`#0E5A3C`)
* **Secondary Accent:** Antique Gold (`#E6C46A`)
* **Text:** Black (`#111111`)

### Additional Tones
* Forest Green (`#1E6B4F`)
* Champagne Gold (`#F4D58D`)
* Soft Beige (`#F6F1E7`)
* Sage Green (`#BFD8C0`)

> [!NOTE]
> Avoid monochrome pages. Alternate backgrounds and accents to create richness.

---

## Placement Rules
* **Use PNG chess pieces:**
  - As faded watermarks
  - In corners
  - Along side borders
  - Near section titles
  - Around graphs
  - Empty states
  - Achievement cards
* **Never place pieces randomly.** Pieces should reinforce meaning.
* Maintain low opacity for decorative pieces.
* Avoid clutter.

---

## Background Rules
* **When section background is white:** Use green and gold chess pieces.
* **When section background is gold:** Use green and white pieces.
* Keep text black.

---

## Page Consistency
Every page should share the same visual language. Use:
- Similar spacing
- Similar card radius
- Similar typography
- Similar section layouts
- Similar button styles
- Similar iconography

Maintain continuity between pages.

---

## Decorative Elements
* **Use:** Crown motifs, stars, gold dividers, subtle chessboard textures, and elegant borders.
* **Never use:** Heavy shadows.
* **Avoid:** Futuristic glassmorphism, neon colors, and gaming aesthetics.

---

## Motion
* Use Framer Motion subtly.
* **Cards:** Should fade upward and animate smoothly.
* **Graphs:** Should draw progressively.
* Hover effects should be elegant and restrained.
* Avoid flashy animations.

---

## General Principle
If a page feels plain, add **elegance**, not complexity.  
If a page feels empty, add **visual storytelling**, not more cards.  
Every screen should feel like a premium chess journal.  
Always prioritize beauty, readability, and consistency.  

*Preserve the current aesthetic and imported font system. These rules are permanent and should guide all future work so that Claude Code and other agents maintain a unified visual identity.*
